Privacy policy

Cover Screen Trackpad, by June8LLC. Last updated 8 August 2026.

Cover Screen Trackpad collects no data whatsoever. It has no analytics, no accounts, no advertising, and makes no network requests. There is no server to send anything to.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not gather, store, transmit or sell any personal or device information. It does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere even in principle.

What stays on your phone

Your settings — pointer size and speed, gesture choices, the active zone, and whether you have unlocked PRO — are saved in the app's private storage on the device. They are never uploaded, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions, and why each one exists

  • Accessibility Service. This is how the app draws the pointer and performs taps, drags and scrolls on your behalf — the same mechanism a mouse would use. It is not used to read screen content for any other purpose, and nothing it observes leaves the device.
  • Display over other apps. Draws the pointer above whatever you are using, so it stays visible as you move between apps.
  • Notifications. Shows the running-session notice, which is also how you stop a session.
  • Shizuku. Used to ask Android to power both screens at once, and to send a camera key if you assign the camera-shutter gesture. Shizuku is a separate app with its own privacy policy.

The app does not request the camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage or internet permissions.

A note on the accessibility permission

Accessibility access is powerful, and you are right to be careful about which apps you grant it to. Cover Screen Trackpad needs it for one reason: injecting pointer movement and clicks is only possible through that API. It reads window state solely to tell whether a keyboard is on screen, so that edge-click scrolling can be suppressed while you type. It does not log, store or transmit anything it sees.

Purchases

The one-time PRO unlock is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see your payment details. The app only records, on your device, whether the purchase exists.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date. Because the app collects nothing, any change would be about clarity rather than about new data collection.
